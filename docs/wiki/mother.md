# MOTHER

MOTHER is the ark's navigation AI. She is represented by six MOTHER cards in a deck. Spent cards appear individually on the ship board.

MOTHER's new role is simple:

> A shared shortcut that can save the ship, steal timing, or push everyone closer to failure.

## What MOTHER Does

When an action stack is short on icons, a contributing player may add MOTHER cards. Each MOTHER card supplies one wild icon.

MOTHER cards drawn into a proposal are temporary. When the proposal resolves, only the needed MOTHER cards are spent and increase the MOTHER used count. If the proposal dissolves, changes, or is abandoned, all unspent MOTHER cards return to the MOTHER Deck immediately.

## What MOTHER Can And Cannot Do

```text
MOTHER can:
- supply missing skill icons on a Star, Gate, or Chamber Build
- complete a stack after at least one human crew has been stacked

MOTHER cannot:
- count as a crew card
- pay Fuel
- pay Parts
- prevent wounds unless a Chamber explicitly says so
- satisfy "also commit +N crew" threshold lines
```

The single rule that gives MOTHER teeth:

> MOTHER may only help if at least one human crew was stacked.

Humans must begin the work; MOTHER finishes it. MOTHER cannot solve a stack on her own.

## Spent Card Count

MOTHER pressure is determined by the number of spent MOTHER cards visible on the ship board. No separate label is needed.

```text
Spent 0-2: no extra effects
Spent 3-4: 3+ ✶ lines active
Spent 5-6: 3+ ✶ and 5+ ✶ lines active; Gates need +1 icon
Need 7th: MOTHER Takes the Wheel - shared loss
```

| Used cards | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 0-2 | none |
| 3-4 | `3+ ✶` lines on cards activate |
| 5-6 | `3+ ✶` and `5+ ✶` lines active; Gates need +1 icon |
| 7th needed | MOTHER Takes the Wheel: shared loss |

## Why Threshold Lines Work

Threshold lines make MOTHER's earlier shortcuts produce future human pressure instead of an abstract corruption modifier.

| Threshold | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Also commit +N crew | A purely human cost. MOTHER cannot pay this. |
| Travel +N Fuel | A real route cost. MOTHER cannot pay this. |
| Add icon X | Need grows by one printed icon. |
| Reward becomes X | Reward is redirected or reduced. |
| Scout N instead | Information shrinks. |

The thesis:

> The more MOTHER performs miracles, the more human presence is required to keep the journey legitimate.

## Strategic Shape

MOTHER is tempting because she always works until she does not. The cost is two-fold: the card stays spent, and the spent-card count may cross a threshold, waking up extra demands on later cards.

Typical pressure points:

```text
Early: "Use MOTHER? It only adds one card to the area."
Mid: "If we use MOTHER, we hit 3 cards and all the 3+ lines wake up."
Late: "If we use MOTHER, Gates get harder and the 7th card loss is close."
```
