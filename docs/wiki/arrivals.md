# Arrivals

After three sectors and three Gates, the ark must dock somewhere. **Three Arrival cards are drawn from the Arrival deck at the Final Approach** — there is no upfront reveal in v2. The table chooses one and attempts it.

Drawing arrivals at the end means players cannot "lock in" a destination before they've crossed three sectors of Stars. They steer their visited Star Legacies toward a *kind* of ending (heavy on Life, on Memory, on Machine) and then see which doors open.

## Arrival card anatomy

```text
NAME
Favored Legacy: one of Life / People / Memory / Machine / Wild
Need: icons (always 5–6)
```

## Examples

```text
GARDEN WORLD
Favored Legacy: Life
Need: Life + Life + Engine + Star + Star
```

```text

```

## Legacy reductions

Every Arrival has a favored Legacy. For each matching Legacy stamp from visited Stars, **remove one icon from the Arrival's Need, up to 3.**

If you installed **Seed Vault**, Life-favored arrivals can reduce up to 4 instead of 3.

That means if you visited four Life-stamped Stars, Garden World drops from 5 icons to 2 icons (or 1 with Seed Vault). The same run makes Archive Moon nearly impossible.

## Resolving the Arrival

Highlight crew (all crew are Ready after the third Gate) and any MOTHER cards you need. Click **Arrive at [name]** on the Arrival you can cover. If none of the three drawn Arrivals can be covered even with everything you have, click any of them to take the **Drift Ending**.

## How Arrivals shape the whole game

Three Arrivals are visible only at the very end, but the Arrival deck has just six cards — so the *kinds* of destinations you might face are knowable. Players quietly steer visited Stars toward a Legacy (or two) that opens many doors.

Note: a **Human Command** ending is only available at 0–2 used MOTHER cards. A run that has already reached the Bent Route band can still arrive, but the ending tone has already crossed into Shared Future.
