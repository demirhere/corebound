# Endings

The ending is read from three things only.

## 1. Destination

Whichever Arrival you docked at. If you couldn't cover any of the three drawn Arrivals, the Drift Ending overrides everything else.

## 2. MOTHER tone

| Used cards | Tone |
|---:|---|
| 0–2 | Human Command |
| 3–4 | Shared Future |
| 5–6 | MOTHER Ascendant |
| 7 | Loss (MOTHER Takes the Wheel) |

The tone bands match the spent MOTHER bands one-to-one: Clear Route → Human Command, Bent Route → Shared Future, Hostile Route → MOTHER Ascendant.

A Human Command ending is therefore impossible if MOTHER has reached even the Bent Route band.

## 3. Dominant Legacy

Look at the Legacy stamps on visited Stars. Whichever Legacy stamp appears most is the dominant one.

| Legacy | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Life | Biology, food, habitats, children, gardens. |
| People | Crew saved, sleepers woken, human bonds protected. |
| Memory | Archives, science, signals, history. |
| Machine | Automation, alien systems, MOTHER routes. |
| Wild | Adapting to strange space instead of controlling it. |

## Formula

```text
Destination + MOTHER tone + Dominant Legacy
```

## Worked endings

> **Garden World + Shared Future + Memory** —
> You reach the Garden World, but the first city is built around the Archive Engine. Humans farm the valleys, while MOTHER curates what must never be forgotten.

> **Free Fleet + Human Command + People** —
> The ark never lands. It becomes a moving civilization, crewed by the awakened and governed by human councils.

> **Archive Moon + MOTHER Ascendant + Machine** —
> You arrive, but not as settlers. MOTHER docks the ark inside the Archive Moon and begins rewriting the mission as preservation, not freedom.

> **Dark Ocean + Shared Future + Wild** —
> The ark sinks into a sea that is not made of water. The crew adapts; MOTHER listens. What surfaces is no longer entirely human.

> **Drift Ending** —
> The Arrival was beyond your reach. The ark drifts forever, kept warm by whatever combination of humans and machine remains.

## Why this works

There is no ending table to memorise. The ending sentence is generated from three values: which Arrival the players picked, which band the MOTHER cards are sitting in, and which Legacy appears most among visited Stars.
